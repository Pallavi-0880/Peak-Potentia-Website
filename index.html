02Academic Research · 6–8 weeks

The Academic Research Program helps students develop strong research skills and intellectual independence — structured guidance in research methodology, topic selection, literature review, data analysis and academic writing, all culminating in a final paper.

Why it matters.

Conducting research as a high-school student enhances your college application by showcasing intellectual curiosity, critical thinking and problem-solving — qualities universities actively seek. Institutions such as Harvard and Stanford emphasise independent research as a marker of readiness for college-level work.

The process lets you dive deeply into a topic of interest, develop expertise and demonstrate initiative and discipline. A completed paper shows admissions officers you're prepared to contribute meaningfully to their academic community.

04AP Test Preparation

College-level rigour, in high school.

Advanced Placement exams are standardised tests that evaluate a student's understanding of college-level coursework while still in high school — a crucial signal for anyone applying to universities in the US and beyond.

High scores strengthen applications and can even earn college credit at top institutions such as Harvard, Stanford and Columbia. We focus on building conceptual understanding first, then layering on exam-oriented technique.
